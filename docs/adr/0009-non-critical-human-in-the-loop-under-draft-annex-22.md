# TraceQ is a non-critical, human-in-the-loop aid under the draft Annex 22 and Annex 11

Status as of 14 November 2026: the revised EU GMP Annex 11, Chapter 4 and the new Annex 22 (Artificial Intelligence) are still drafts. The joint EC/PIC/S consultation ran from July to October 2025 ([EC consultation page](https://health.ec.europa.eu/consultations/stakeholders-consultation-eudralex-volume-4-good-manufacturing-practice-guidelines-chapter-4-annex_en)). [EudraLex Volume 4](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medicinal-products/eudralex/eudralex-volume-4_en) still holds the 2011 Annex 11 and no Annex 22. The EMA inspectors working group targets final texts for Q4 2026 ([work plan 2026–2028](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/other/3-year-work-plan-inspectors-working-group_en.pdf)). The [draft Annex 22](https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/5f38a92d-bb8e-4264-8898-ea076e926db6_en?filename=mp_vol4_chap4_annex22_consultation_guideline_en.pdf) §1 says it "does not apply to Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLM), and such models should not be used in critical GMP applications". It allows them in non-critical applications "which do not have direct impact on patient safety, product quality or data integrity", provided qualified personnel are responsible for the outputs (human-in-the-loop). A later [EMA workshop report](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/report/report-multistakeholder-workshop-expert-contributions-artificial-intelligence-guidance-development-annex-22_en.pdf) (October 2026) says the drafting group discussed widening the scope to LLMs under a risk-based control strategy, so the final text may differ. BPOM (CPOB Annex 7, PerBPOM 7/2024) follows the 2011 Annex 11 and has no AI guidance.

We position TraceQ on the non-critical, human-in-the-loop path. This extends ADR 0002 and does not replace it.

- **Every LLM output is a draft proposal:** the traceability matrix, gaps, Pre-review findings and drafted Test Steps. None of it is a GMP record. A record exists only after a qualified engineer reviews and approves it, and the plant stays accountable for it (draft Chapter 4 §4.24).
- **Public claim:** "designed against the July 2025 draft Annex 22 and Annex 11; final texts pending". TraceQ never claims to be compliant with Annex 22.
- **Risks we state openly:**
  - The draft does not define "direct impact on … data integrity", and an approved traceability matrix is a GMP record. Whether drafting its content counts as non-critical use is a judgement for the plant's QA.
  - A requirement the LLM misses is hard for a reviewer to see, and reviewers may approve by habit (automation bias). The design must answer this: every URS requirement appears in the matrix by construction and needs a human decision, and an undecided state is shown rather than forced into a class.
- **Review trigger:** revisit this ADR when EudraLex Volume 4 publishes the final Annex 22 or Annex 11.

## Consequences

- Each AI Finding records the AI proposal, the human's final decision, who decided and why it changed. These records are the human-in-the-loop evidence, and AI-proposed versus human-final agreement is a metric of its own.
- Draft Annex 22 §5.6 calls test data or labels generated by generative AI "not recommended" unless "fully justified". The justification is design §2 principle 7 (no LLM-chosen value frozen without human review), and only human-decided labels back a published claim (design §9).
- A model or prompt change is a change-control event that requires a live eval before release (design §9, "Running"). Model versions are pinned.
