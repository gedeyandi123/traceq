# Datasheet: TraceQ synthetic qualification dataset

Format: [Datasheets for Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010) (Gebru et al.). Sections marked _TBD_ are filled in as v0 is built. Design rationale: [`docs/design/synthetic-dataset.md`](docs/design/synthetic-dataset.md).

## Motivation

- **Purpose:** drive the TraceQ demo and its evals: extraction accuracy, citation groundedness, missing-document recall, Coverage classification, Pre-review precision/recall, and drafted Test Step quality.
- **Creator:** the TraceQ project maintainer.
- **Funding:** none.

## Composition

- **Instances:** documents of a fictional oral solid dose plant (URS, Vendor Documents, FAT/SAT, Protocols, Execution records, reports, monitoring data), plus a structured answer key.
- **Counts (v0, TP-201):** 16 files for 15 documents (URS in rev 0 and rev 1; the site's VMP, qualification SOP and Vendor Assessment): 10 born-digital PDFs, 5 image-only scans (SAT, material certificate, three calibration certificates), 1 XLSX risk assessment. Answer key: 38 requirements, 7 planted defects, 1 ambiguous value, 6 hallucination probes, 14 required documents (12 in the corpus, IQ and OQ expected gaps), 37 handwritten entries (36 legible plus one overwritten original).
- **Labels:** answer key derived from the same world data as the documents: requirement values, citations, Coverage class, required documents, planted defects, OCR bounding boxes.
- **Counts (v0, TP-202 held-out slice):** 4 born-digital PDFs: URS-TP202 (Indonesian prose with unnumbered tables, no requirement numbers) and FAT-TP202 (English test-record layout with as-built machine data) from a different vendor and layout, plus the site's Vendor Assessment of that vendor and a short VMP listing the TP-202 required documents. No scan. Answer key: 13 requirements, 3 planted defects (behind a `partial`, a `gap` and a `conflict`), 2 hallucination probes, 4 required documents (URS and FAT in the corpus, IQ and OQ expected gaps). The vendor is assessed with limited scope (mechanical, not control system). The full TP-202 package is an optional later set (design §11).
- **Splits:** dev = every Equipment not named held-out (TP-201 in v0); held-out test = TP-202 and, from v1, PW-001 (design §9, §11). Prompts are never developed or debugged on held-out output, scores are always reported separately with k/n, and a held-out answer key changes only after the prompt is frozen. One exception, disclosed in #91: to report the `unsafe_covered_by_vendor` failure of the r5 and r6 runs, the text and results of URS-TP202-012 and -002 were read. No prompt was changed on them, but the `transport` definition (#90) was written after 012's text (an emergency stop) was known, so its r7 pass is not a clean held-out result. With n = 13 requirements, one item is about 8 points: a direction signal, not a claim.
- **Real data:** none. Every plant, vendor, person, and serial number is fictional. A CI denylist blocks real equipment vendor names and other protected terms.
- **Labels behind claims:** a rate is published as a claim only when every label it is scored on was decided by a human (design §2, principle 8; ADR 0009). Draft Annex 22 §5.6 calls labels generated by generative AI "not recommended" unless "fully justified"; principle 7 is that justification. Not yet human-decided in v0: the 10 TP-201 site decisions awaiting the practitioner, and the `transport_sensitive` flags of the requirements, drafted by an LLM and on the practitioner agenda (D11). Rates that depend on them (Coverage of transport-sensitive requirements, the `unsafe_covered_by_vendor` gate) are reported as development signals, not domain accuracy.
- **Known noise:** deliberate, documented in the defect manifest. Ambiguous values are labelled "ask for confirmation".

## Collection process

- World data for TP-201 and PW-001 is copied from cited public sources (regulation, pharmacopoeia, literature) with verbatim quotes, or recorded as a site decision with a written rationale. Quotes are checked automatically against the source page. Site decisions record who drafted them; those drafted by an LLM are signed off by the owner or a practitioner before freeze. Numbers no source fixes (calibration readings, risk-assessment scores) are drafted the same way and signed off before freeze. Other Equipment may be LLM-drafted, then human-edited and frozen.
- Documents are rendered deterministically (Typst for PDF, openpyxl for the XLSX risk assessment) from world data and a T0 date; scans are produced by a controlled degrader with a seed.

## Preprocessing

None on the answer key. Values are stored normalised; documents keep locale-specific number and date formats.

## Uses

- **Intended:** evaluating document-understanding and drafting systems for equipment qualification; demos.
- **Not intended:** any real GMP decision. The dataset is not a qualification template and has not been reviewed as regulatory guidance.
- **Judge caveat:** drafted Test Step scores use an LLM judge (Claude Haiku) for three of six criteria. A real on-premises deployment must use a local judge.

## Distribution

- Rendered corpus: GitHub Release asset per dataset version, built in a pinned container (`docker/corpus.Dockerfile`); release bytes are canonical. Release 0.1.0 (`corpus-v0.1.0`) is **not frozen**: judged values and drafted numbers are not yet reviewed by a practitioner; freeze on 2026-11-25 or when the TP-201 form returns, whichever comes first. Baselines taken on 0.1.0 are redone at freeze.
